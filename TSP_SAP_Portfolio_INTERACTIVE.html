The dated migration

SAP Business Suite 7 mainstream maintenance ends in 2027.

Extended maintenance runs to 2030 at additional cost, and SAP has committed S/4HANA maintenance through 2040. For anyone still on ECC, the decision window is the one TSP's assessment and conversion tooling was built for — Kelvin and ATLAS read what the business actually uses before a plan is written, AutoMig and TestGenius compress the build and the test cycle after it.
